FBI Disrupts QTFY Infrastructure (QScan/QTRouter) Used Against U.S. Organizations

來自中國（歸因於中國的攻擊組織） 置信度: 高

The U.S. Department of Justice announced the disruption of two hacking platforms, QScan and QTRouter, operated by a Chinese state-sponsored threat actor known as QTFY, employed by Nanjing Xinjiuwei Network Technology Company. The platforms were used to target critical infrastructure and other sensitive networks in the United States and steal data from U.S. organizations.
