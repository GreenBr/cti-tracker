US disrupts QTFY's QScan and QTRouter platforms targeting critical infrastructure

來自中國（歸因於中國的攻擊組織） 置信度: 高

U.S. authorities disrupted QScan and QTRouter, two platforms operated by China-linked group QTFY to target U.S. critical infrastructure and government networks. QScan infected internet-connected devices, while QTRouter used compromised systems to conceal the origin of intrusions targeting agencies including NASA, the Federal Reserve, and the Department of Energy.
