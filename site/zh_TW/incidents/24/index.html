Chinese-speaking actor (Earth Berberoka-linked) hijacks Brazilian government sites for SEO fraud and phishing

來自中國（歸因於中國的攻擊組織） 置信度: 中

Since mid-2025, a Chinese-speaking cybercrime group whose tradecraft links it to Earth Berberoka has compromised dozens of Brazilian websites, most of them .gov.br federal, state and municipal government domains, and installed custom Apache modules (opsproxy and a second RC4-configured content-injection module) that transparently proxy visitors to attacker-hosted phishing pages impersonating Google Play, Microsoft Store and Amazon, primarily promoting gambling/betting content. The group deploys a broader Linux toolkit including a Go downloader (DownPro), a setuid privilege-escalation backdoor (chuser), a credential stealer, and a Go-based reconnaissance/scanning agent (cam-agent); installer scripts, backdoor help text and phishing-page CSS comments contain Chinese-language strings.
