Fire Ant implants on Cisco IOS XR routers used for stealth, packet capture, and exfiltration

来自中国（归因于中国的攻击组织） 置信度: 中

Sygnia reported that the China-nexus threat actor Fire Ant deployed purpose-built implants on Cisco IOS XR routers that suppressed syslog delivery, filtered show command output, and maintained a hidden GRE tunnel. The actor captured router packet traffic, exfiltrated it to external FTP servers, and conducted connection attempts and port scans against connected systems tied to critical infrastructure; Sygnia did not determine the initial access vector.
