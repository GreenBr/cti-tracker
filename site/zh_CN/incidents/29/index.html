QN Wallpaper DLL sideloading chain delivers ValleyRAT (Silver Fox)

来自中国（归因于中国的攻击组织） 置信度: 中

Kaspersky detailed a malicious installer that deploys a trojanized version of the legitimate Chinese adware tool QN Wallpaper to perform DLL sideloading and deliver the ValleyRAT backdoor, which captures keystrokes and clipboard contents and resists termination. Kaspersky attributed the activity to Silver Fox, describing it as motivated by both cyber espionage and financial gain and targeting organizations across multiple countries.
