Chinese-speaking actor exploits ownCloud flaw to breach Philippine nuclear research body

来自中国（归因于中国的攻击组织） 置信度: 中

A Chinese-speaking threat actor exploited CVE-2023-49105, a critical authentication bypass vulnerability in ownCloud, to compromise a nuclear research institution in the Philippines and steal records. CISA subsequently added the vulnerability to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.
