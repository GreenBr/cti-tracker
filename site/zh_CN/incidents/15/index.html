Silver Fox distributes ValleyRAT via trojanized QN Wallpaper adware using DLL sideloading

来自中国（归因于中国的攻击组织） 置信度: 中

Kaspersky analyzed an installer disguising the ValleyRAT (Winos 4.0) backdoor as a modified copy of the legitimate Chinese adware QN Wallpaper. The installer sideloads a malicious libcef.dll into the signed QnWallpaper.exe process, disables Windows Defender, adds autorun persistence, self-elevates via runas, and marks its process as critical to resist termination; Kaspersky assessed Silver Fox as the likely operator based on geography and payload.
