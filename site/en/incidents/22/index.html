Fire Ant (UNC3886) Expands to Trusted Network Infrastructure in 2026

From China (PRC-attributed actor) Confidence: High

The China-linked threat actor Fire Ant (aka UNC3886) compromised routers (including Cisco IOS XR), TACACS servers, authentication systems, and Linux management hosts to maintain covert access, collect credentials and traffic, and pivot into connected high-value and third-party environments. The group deployed the Medusa rootkit, custom SSH backdoors, Zabbix-masquerading malware (BridgeAgent), and a TACACS credential-collection tool (TacTap), while suppressing router logging and manipulating command output and host logs to evade detection.
