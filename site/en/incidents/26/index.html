Gambling Goblin (Earth Berberoka) hijacks Brazilian government Apache servers for gambling SEO fraud

From China (PRC-attributed actor) Confidence: Medium

A Chinese-speaking cybercrime cluster tracked by Check Point as Gambling Goblin, and linked to the actor Earth Berberoka, has since mid-2025 installed malicious Apache modules on compromised Brazilian government and educational web servers, reverse-proxying visitors to gambling/betting phishing pages disguised as app stores to manipulate search rankings. The group deployed tools including the DownPro downloader, AlphaAgent backdoor, oRAT, a 3snake-based SSH/sudo credential stealer, an SSH brute-forcer, and a Go-based reconnaissance/scanning agent.
