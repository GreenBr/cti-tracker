QTFY campaign targeting U.S. federal agencies and critical infrastructure via ORB proxy network

From China (PRC-attributed actor) Confidence: Medium

QTFY, a China-based technical quartermaster affiliated with the Ministry of State Security, used its QScan vulnerability-scanning platform and QTRouter obfuscation network to target U.S. federal agencies (NASA, Federal Reserve, DOE, DOJ, HHS, NIH, U.S. Senate) as well as hospitals, telecom operators, power companies, financial institutions, and defense contractors. The FBI seized domains tied to QScan and QTRouter (qtproxy[.]xyz, qt-proxy[.]org, qt-team[.]com) to disrupt the operation.
