Fire Ant expands campaign to Cisco IOS XR routers, TACACS servers, and Linux hosts

From China (PRC-attributed actor) Confidence: Medium

China-nexus actor Fire Ant expanded a long-running intrusion beyond VMware ESXi/vCenter to compromise Cisco IOS XR routers, TACACS authentication servers, and Linux management hosts, deploying new tools TacTap (a tac_plus library-injection credential collector) and BridgeAgent (a Zabbix-masquerading backdoor) alongside Medusa/REPTILE rootkits to capture traffic, steal credentials, and suppress logging. Sygnia assessed strong but inconclusive overlap with UNC3886, and found limited scanning/probing (not confirmed compromise) against connected critical infrastructure.
